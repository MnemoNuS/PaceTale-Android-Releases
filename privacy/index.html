Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

PaceTale is a running companion being developed for invitation-only testing. This policy explains the data involved in using PaceTale and how to contact the project about it.

Contact

PaceTale is responsible for the processing described in this policy. For privacy questions or requests about your data, write to evgeniyleshin@gmail.com.

Data involved

The public landing page and registration guide are hosted on ChatGPT Sites and have no account form. The hosting service receives technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect those pages. The invitation link opens a separate PaceTale page on Railway.

  • Your email address and invitation information, to manage access to the test.
  • When Google sign-in is enabled and you choose it, Google account identity and email information used to identify you and sign you in. Google may also provide profile details such as your name and profile picture. Google handles your Google password.
  • Account preferences, selected content and run history and results, so the app can provide and restore your experience.
  • Location and run measurements, when you grant the relevant device permission and use features that need them. Some run data is kept on your device; optional telemetry is subject to your choices in the app.
  • Technical and security information generated when the service handles requests, to operate and protect the service.

Cookies on the public website

Our hosting providers may set the following cookies when you visit pacetale.com.

  • __cf_bm is set by Cloudflare to help detect automated traffic. It expires after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • cf_clearance is set by Cloudflare to remember the result of a security check. In our checks, it was set to expire after one year.
  • __Host-appgarden-visitor is set by ChatGPT Sites on page load and expires after 90 days. OpenAI classifies it as an analytics cookie used to measure site visits.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking security cookies may cause Cloudflare to repeat a check. See the OpenAI cookie policy and Cloudflare cookie documentation. The APK download link opens GitHub, which applies its own privacy practices.

How data is used and shared

We use this information to manage invitations and sign-in, provide runs, maintain your account and run history, troubleshoot and protect the service. Google provides the sign-in service; Supabase provides authentication and database services; Railway hosts application services. These providers process information as needed to provide their services.

Google account information is used for account identification and sign-in. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Information may be stored on your device and by service providers operating the test. We use HTTPS for network connections and access controls for account and administrative functions. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and requests

Account and run data are kept during the test. To request access to or deletion of your information, email us at the address above. We handle requests manually and may need to verify your identity before acting. Device-only and unsynchronized information may require action on your device. We will explain any data that must be retained and any remaining steps in our response.

Changes

We may update this policy as PaceTale develops. We will update this page when our practices change.
