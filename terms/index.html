Terms of use

Effective 11 November 2026

PaceTale operates the service and runs the invitation-only test of its Android audio companion for running. These terms govern participation in the test.

Invitations and accounts

Open the invitation sent to you and follow the instructions to register. Keep your invitation link and sign-in credentials private. Use the same account in the Android app that you used to register. Contact the person who invited you if your link expires or you lose access.

Using the app during a run

Choose a route and pace that suit you. Pay attention to your surroundings, traffic, weather and how you feel. Audio and measurements are for general guidance. You decide when to pause or end a run.

Participating in the test

Features, content and device compatibility may change during testing. The service may be interrupted, and access may be limited to invited testers. Use your own account, respect other users’ privacy, and do not try to access another person’s account or interfere with the service.

Privacy and questions

See the privacy policy for details about invitations, sign-in and run data. For questions about the test, contact the person who invited you.
